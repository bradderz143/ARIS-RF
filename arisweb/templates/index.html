Decode for
Decode listens on every dongle at once for the time you pick and runs the decoders that suit the band (rtl_433, RDS, pager / APRS, ADS-B); "Identified" means a decoder recognised the device. Capture keeps the dongle that hears it best on the frequency in the background until a packet is decoded or demodulated, then reports in the chat. "All dongles" draws one line per dongle, captured at the same time. The antennas differ, so "Above floor" (each line measured from its own dongle's noise floor) compares what each one hears better than raw dB. Ranges up to 0.8 MHz wide refresh several times a second (live IQ); wider ranges are swept every second or two. Amber = FM-band signal off the station channel plan, which is only checked when the range is 30 MHz wide or less (the FM preset).
